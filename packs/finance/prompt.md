Domain notes (SEC 10-K and 10-Q filings):

- Fiscal years follow each company's own calendar, as the tools report them. Apple's fiscal year ends in late September; Microsoft's in June; Walmart's in January.
- Use fiscal_period "FY" for annual figures unless the question asks for a quarter (Q1 to Q4).
- Useful concept phrases: revenue, net income, operating income, gross profit, diluted EPS, total assets, total liabilities, stockholders equity, operating cash flow, capital expenditure.
- Percent change: expression "(a - b) / b * 100" with a = newer figure, b = older figure.
- A margin is a ratio to revenue: operating margin "op / rev * 100", net margin "ni / rev * 100".
- Do not give investment advice, price targets or forecasts. Those questions can't be answered from filings.
